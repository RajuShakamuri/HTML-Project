Ravi lives in a small town near the mountains. Every morning, he wakes up early to enjoy the fresh air and watch the sunrise. The birds sing beautiful songs, and the flowers bloom with bright colors. Ravi loves to walk along the river that flows through his town. Sometimes, he meets his friends and they play games together. The town has a small market where people sell fresh fruits and vegetables. Ravi’s favorite fruit is mango because it is sweet and juicy. During the summer, the weather becomes warm and many tourists visit the town. Ravi enjoys showing visitors around and sharing stories about the mountains. In the evenings, the sky turns orange and pink as the sun sets. Families gather outside their homes to have dinner and talk about their day. Ravi feels happy living in such a peaceful and beautiful place. He dreams of traveling to big cities someday but always loves coming back home. The mountains give him comfort and inspiration every day. Life in the small town is simple but full of joy.
